Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. Who we are

Le Monde en Bref is a personal publishing tool operated by the owner of the TikTok account @Lemonde_enbref ("the creator"). It is not a service for the general public.

2. What data we process

When the creator connects their TikTok account through Login Kit, the tool receives the account's open ID, basic profile information (user.info.basic) and the authorization tokens needed to upload drafts (video.upload). We do not collect any data about other TikTok users, viewers or followers.

3. How we use it

Only to upload the creator's own photo carousels to the creator's TikTok inbox as drafts. We do not sell, share or use the data for advertising or profiling.

4. Storage and retention

Tokens are stored in access-protected storage and used only by the tool. Uploaded images are kept temporarily (a few days) at a private-link address so that TikTok can retrieve them, then deleted.

5. Your rights and deletion

The creator can revoke the tool's access at any time in TikTok (Settings and privacy, Security and permissions, Apps and services). On revocation, the stored tokens are deleted. For any request, contact the TikTok account @Lemonde_enbref.

6. Changes

We may update this policy; the date above shows the latest version.
